About me

Building AI systems that can earn trust.

I am a B.Tech student in Computer Science and Engineering - Artificial Intelligence at the Indian Institute of Information Technology, Design and Manufacturing, Kancheepuram. My research sits at the intersection of trustworthy language models, agentic reasoning and autonomous planning, multimodal computational neuroscience, and privacy-preserving AI.

Across these areas, I return to one question: how can an AI system show that its answer, plan, or prediction deserves trust? I build systems where evidence, verification, deterministic execution, or structured representations are part of the pipeline - not an afterthought.

Research direction

From fluent output to reliable action.

I study trust at multiple levels: whether a model cites the right evidence, constructs valid plans, grounds reasoning in the correct modality, and remains useful when the original data cannot be shared.

01 / ATTRIBUTION

Trustworthy language models

I evaluate how retrieval, citation timing, and evidence coverage shape the trustworthiness of LLM answers, particularly in domains where claims must remain human-verifiable.

When does an answer become trustworthy: when it sounds right, or when every important claim can be traced?

02 / PLANNING

Agentic reasoning & autonomous planning

I explore agents that synthesize reusable procedures and execute them deterministically, moving expensive reasoning from every test instance into a verifiable development process.

Can an LLM build the solver once, then let a deterministic program solve the next thousand problems?

03 / MULTIMODAL

Computational neuroscience

I work with aligned video, audio, language, and fMRI signals to study how multimodal representations can predict brain activity evoked by naturalistic stimuli.

Which representations preserve enough of a movie's meaning to explain the brain's response?

04 / ROBUSTNESS

Privacy-preserving & secure AI

I investigate synthetic sensing and frequency-domain anomaly detection for systems where privacy, unseen environments, and cyber-physical safety constrain ordinary data collection.

How can models learn useful structure without exposing the people or systems that generated the data?

Long-term vision

AI that knows when to look, reason, verify, and act.

My long-term goal is to develop knowledge-infused AI systems whose internal process is inspectable and whose outputs can be checked before they affect the world.

GroundConnect claims to evidence, sensors, and structured knowledge.
ReasonForm plans and explanations that respect constraints.
VerifyUse executors, metrics, and humans to test validity.
ActDeploy only when uncertainty and failure modes are visible.

PlanForge: Synthesizing Planners from Natural Language with an LLM-Driven Architect-Builder-Runner

Sarvesh Baskar*, Raviteja Bommireddy*, Saksham Kumar Sharma, Harsha Kokel, Manas Gaur, Michael Katz, Shirin Sohrabi, Kavitha Srinivas

IBM Research · UMBC · IIITDM Kancheepuram

Question: Can an LLM synthesize a reusable planner instead of reasoning from scratch for every instance?

PlanForge separates an Architect, Builder, and deterministic Runner. The resulting planners reach 100% success on NaturalPlan and StructureSAT, 92.69% VAL-verified accuracy on Plan Gen, and require no test-time API calls.

DIAGRAMS: A Review Framework for Reasoning-Level Attribution in Diagram QA

Anirudh Iyengar Kaniyar Narayana Iyengar, Tampu Ravi Kumar, Manan Suri, Raviteja Bommireddy, Dinesh Manocha, Puneet Mathur, Vivek Gupta

Proceedings of ACL 2026, System Demonstrations · pages 774-784

Question: Which visual regions are actually necessary to support every step of a diagram answer?

DIAGRAMS provides a schema-driven, human-in-the-loop review framework across six Diagram QA datasets. Model-suggested evidence reaches 85.39% precision and 75.30% recall against reviewer-final selections.

2026 Preprint arXiv

PhaseNet++: Phase-Aware Frequency-Domain Anomaly Detection for Industrial Control Systems via Phase Coherence Graphs

Raviteja Bommireddy, Varshith Bandaru, Lohith Pakala, Pradeep Kumar B

arXiv:2605.00929 [cs.LG]

Question: What do industrial anomaly detectors miss when they discard phase information?

PhaseNet++ turns pairwise phase coherence into a graph and jointly reconstructs magnitude and phase. On SWaT, it reaches 90.98% F1, 95.66% ROC-AUC, and 91.51% average precision.

2025 Workshop NeurIPS

Generation-Time vs. Post-hoc Citation: A Holistic Evaluation of LLM Attribution

Yash Saxena, Raviteja Bommireddy, Ankur Padia, Manas Gaur

NeurIPS 2025 LLM Evaluation Workshop · arXiv:2509.21557

Question: Should an LLM cite while writing, or draft first and attach evidence afterward?

Across four attribution datasets, retrieval is the main driver of citation quality. Post-hoc citation offers stronger coverage with competitive correctness, motivating a retrieval-centric, P-Cite-first strategy for high-stakes use.
